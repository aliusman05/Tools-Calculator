## Phase 10, first batch

Refinance break-even, student loan payoff and rent vs buy were added, each with independently derived
reference values, tests for the main scenarios and every input assumption, and a page that states what it
does not cover. Test-suite changes made along the way: calculators in the shared specs now come from the
registry; the timing tests run in their own project after everything else; the INP guard follows the real
INP definition and is shown to catch an injected slowdown; the e2e helper waits for hydration, which
removed the WebKit and phone flakiness.

### Take-home pay (US federal)

Federal income tax (reusing the income tax engine), Social Security with its wage cap, Medicare and the
Additional Medicare tax, traditional 401(k), pre-tax benefits and after-tax deductions, in a dated data
file with sources. State and local taxes are out of scope and stated on the page. The headline is an
average over the year; for pay above the Social Security cap the page also shows the early and later
paychecks, and says that Additional Medicare withholding timing makes the later ones a little smaller.
The payroll data was compared with IRS and SSA pages on 2026-10-19 but is not yet signed off by you
(`ownerVerified`); the launch check shows a warning until you do. 2027 figures are not published yet:
see [TAX-YEAR-ROLLOVER.md](TAX-YEAR-ROLLOVER.md).

## What was fixed in Phase 9

- Security headers, preview deployments `noindex`, brand icons, and removal of the create-next-app files.
- GA4 that sends only the page path (the address holds typed values) and loads only after consent.
- Home page mobile score: the cookie banner was the LCP element; it is now server-rendered (94 to 96).
- Cross-browser test bugs (not site bugs) found by the new Firefox, WebKit and phone suites.
