## Results are estimates

Every result depends on the numbers and assumptions you enter, and on simplifications we make so the calculators stay understandable. For example:

- Loan calculators assume a fixed rate and equal monthly payments, and they do not include every fee a lender might charge.
- Savings and retirement projections assume a constant rate of return. Real returns vary from year to year and can be negative. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
- The income tax estimator covers US federal tax on ordinary income only. It leaves out payroll taxes, state and local taxes, capital gains rates, and many deductions, credits and phase-outs.
- The inflation calculator applies a constant rate that you choose. It does not use historical price data.

Your actual results will differ. Your lender, plan administrator or tax return is the authority for your own numbers, and a qualified professional can advise you on your circumstances.
