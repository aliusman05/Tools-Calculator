## The short version

- **Your calculator inputs stay in your browser.** Every calculation runs on your device. The amounts, rates and ages you type are not sent to our servers and are not stored by us.

- **Optional analytics and advertising cookies are off until you say yes.** You can accept, reject or customize them, and change your mind at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer.

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect or store the financial figures you enter into a calculator. We do not require registration, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive information such as government identifiers, bank details or payment card numbers. Please do not include such information in messages to us.

## Information that is collected

### Calculator links

The numbers you type into a calculator stay in your browser while you use it. They are not added to the address of the page, and we do not send them to our servers. If you press **Copy link**, the link that is copied contains the values you entered, so that you or someone you send it to can see the same scenario. Those values appear in an address only because you chose to copy and share it. When someone opens such a link, their browser sends the full address to our hosting provider, as it does for every web request, and it may appear in standard server logs. The page reads the values once and then removes them from the address bar. We do not use these logs to identify or profile you. Share a link only if you are comfortable with the person you share it with seeing those values.

### Server logs

Our hosting provider and content delivery network process technical information needed to serve the site and keep it secure, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request. These logs are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

### Messages you send us

If you use the contact form or email us, we receive your name, your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you and to improve the site. We keep messages for as long as needed to deal with your request and then delete them, normally within 12 months, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

### Your cookie choice

We store your cookie choice in your browser's local storage so that we do not ask you again on every visit. It contains your selections and the date, and is never used to identify you.

## Cookies and similar technologies

We group the technologies we use into three categories:

- **Strictly necessary.** Security and remembering your cookie choice. These do not need consent.
- **Analytics.** If you accept, we may use Google Analytics to understand which pages and calculators are used, in aggregate. These cookies are not set unless you opt in. We send Google only the page path, never the part of the address after the question mark, so the numbers you type into a calculator are not included.
- **Advertising.** If you accept, we and our advertising partners, including Google AdSense, may use cookies and similar identifiers to show and measure ads, including personalized ads based on your browsing. These are not set unless you opt in.

We use Google Consent Mode, so Google's tags only receive the permissions you have given. If you reject a category, the related tags do not store or read cookies for it.

The site is supported by advertising and, in places, affiliate links. Our advertising partners may collect and use data as described in their own policies. The address of a calculator page does not contain the values you type, so advertising partners do not receive them from the page address. The exception is a shared link that you open: its address briefly contains the shared values before the page removes them (see "Calculator links" above). If that concerns you, choose Reject all in Cookie settings. For Google, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can manage personalized advertising from Google at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com), and from participating companies at [youradchoices.com](https://youradchoices.com) (United States) or [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu) (Europe).

When you click an affiliate link, the partner may set its own cookies to record the referral. Affiliate links are marked wherever they appear. See our [disclaimer](/disclaimer) for details.
