## How to use this calculator

1. Type the **loan amount** you plan to borrow.
2. Enter the **interest rate** from your offer or a rate you want to test.
3. Choose the **term** in years or months. A 60-month loan and a 5-year loan give identical results.
4. Set the **first payment** month so the schedule shows real dates and your payoff month.
5. Add an **extra monthly payment** if you want to see how paying a little more speeds things up.

## How it works

The monthly payment for a loan with equal payments is:

```
M = P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n − 1)
```

- **P** is the loan amount.
- **r** is the monthly rate: the annual rate divided by 12 and by 100.
- **n** is the number of monthly payments.

At a rate of zero the formula reduces to P ÷ n. Each month, interest is the current balance times r, and whatever remains of the payment reduces the balance. The last payment is adjusted so the balance finishes at exactly zero. Extra payments are applied to principal in full, so they cut the number of months needed.

## Worked example

You borrow **$25,000** at **6.9%** for **5 years** (60 payments).

- Monthly payment: **$493.85**.
- In the first month, **$143.75** is interest and **$350.10** reduces the balance.
- Total interest over the loan: **$4,631.08**, for **$29,631.08** paid in total.

Add **$100 extra each month** and the loan ends after **49 payments** instead of 60. Interest falls to **$3,706.88**, which saves **$924.20** and **11 months**.

## Tips and common mistakes

- **Compare total cost, not just the payment.** A lower payment from a longer term often costs more in interest.
- **Check for fees.** Origination fees and late fees are not included here, so add them to your own comparison.
- **Confirm how extra payments are applied.** Ask your lender to apply them to principal, not to the next due date.
- **Use the APR your lender quotes for the loan itself.** Avoid mixing a promotional teaser rate with a long term.
- **Test a rate change.** If your rate is variable, try a point or two higher to see whether you are still comfortable.
